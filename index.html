    How to play
    • Type any character from the list and press Enter. You have unlimited guesses, but fewer is better.
    • Partly right (amber) means some of it matches: Bloom's home realm is Domino and Earth, so guessing her when the answer is from Earth gives amber.
    • Affinity has three levels. Evil is for the main villains (the Trix, Darkar, Valtor, the Black Circle, Tritannus and so on). Bad is for lesser troublemakers like Mitzi, Diaspro and Amentia. Everyone else is Good. Bad and Evil count as partly right for each other.
    • Debut counts the first two films as half-seasons: Film 1 sits between seasons 3 and 4, Film 2 between seasons 4 and 5.
    • Typing a main character's name also suggests the lesser-known people tied to them. Type Bloom and you'll see her parents, her adoptive parents, Kiko and Mitzi.
    • After 4 guesses you can reveal the character's power or role. After 7 you can reveal their first letter.
    • Daily gives everyone the same character each day (it changes at midnight). Endless lets you play as many rounds as you want.
